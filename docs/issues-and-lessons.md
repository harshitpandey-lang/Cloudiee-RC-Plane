## Key Learnings from This Project

This project provided practical experience in:

- Aircraft design
- Aerodynamics
- Embedded electronics
- Power systems
- RC communication
- Structural engineering
- Flight testing
- Troubleshooting and iteration

More importantly, it demonstrates that successful engineering rarely works perfectly on the first attempt. Many improvements came directly from failures observed during testing and subsequent design iterations.

## Material Selection Impact

One of the biggest lessons learned was the critical importance of material selection:

- **Sunboard (Heavier):** More durable and easier to work with, but significantly heavier
- **Depron (Lighter):** Better flight characteristics but more fragile during construction
- **Trade-offs:** Future iterations will prioritize weight optimization over ease of construction

## Weight Management

The aircraft's inability to achieve sustained flight was primarily due to:
- Heavy sunboard material choice
- Electronics and battery weight accumulation
- Insufficient wing area for the total mass
- Under-powered motor for the final aircraft weight

## Future Improvements

Planned upgrades to increase performance:
- Rebuild using 5mm Depron for significant weight reduction
- Increase wing area to improve lift generation
- Consider more powerful motor/ESC combination
- Implement telemetry and FPV systems
- Add autonomous capabilities with waypoint navigation
