# Issues Encountered and Lessons Learned

## Introduction

Since Cloudiee is my first RC aircraft project, many design decisions were made through experimentation and learning. Throughout the build process I encountered several challenges related to material selection, aircraft weight, electronics integration and overall aircraft design.

Rather than hiding these problems, this document records them along with the lessons learned from each stage of development.

---

## Issue 1 – Excessive Aircraft Weight

### Problem

The aircraft was initially built using sunboard as the primary construction material.

Although the material was easy to work with and structurally strong, it turned out to be significantly heavier than expected.

The wing alone weighed approximately 346 grams before accounting for:

- Motor
- ESC
- Battery
- Receiver
- Servos
- Wiring
- Fuselage

This resulted in an aircraft weight that may exceed the amount of lift generated by the wing.

### Cause

I prioritised structural strength over weight reduction during the design phase.

At the time I had limited experience with aircraft materials and underestimated the importance of weight in fixed-wing aircraft.

### Lesson Learned

Aircraft design is heavily dependent on weight management.

Reducing weight is often more beneficial than increasing structural strength.

For future aircraft I will use:

- Depron Foam
- Styrofoam
- Lightweight Foamboard

instead of dense sunboard.

## Issue 2 – Limited Knowledge of Aircraft Design

### Problem

This was my first RC aircraft project.

Many decisions regarding:

- Wing dimensions
- Fuselage sizing
- Electronics placement
- Structural design

were made while learning the concepts simultaneously.

### Cause

Lack of previous experience with RC aviation.

### Lesson Learned

Practical building teaches concepts far better than theory alone.

Building an aircraft from scratch exposed me to:

- Lift generation
- Aircraft stability
- Weight distribution
- Structural reinforcement
- Aerodynamic considerations

which would have been difficult to fully understand through research only.

---

## Issue 3 – Electronics Integration

### Problem

Connecting all components correctly required careful planning.

Components included:

- ESC
- Brushless Motor
- Battery
- Receiver
- Servos
- XT60 Connectors

Incorrect wiring could potentially damage electronics.

### Solution

Before permanently installing components I tested:

- Servo operation
- Motor response
- Receiver binding
- Power connections

individually.

### Lesson Learned

Testing subsystems separately greatly reduces troubleshooting effort later.

Future projects will follow a similar approach.

---

## Issue 4 – Aircraft Transport

### Problem

A single-piece wing would make transportation difficult.

### Solution

The wing was designed to be detachable from the fuselage.

### Lesson Learned

Ease of maintenance and transportation should be considered during the design stage rather than after construction.

---

## Issue 5 – Documentation Delay

### Problem

Most attention was initially focused on construction.

Documentation was started later in the project.

### Solution

A GitHub repository was created to properly record:

- Design decisions
- Build process
- Electronics
- Images
- Videos
- Lessons learned

### Lesson Learned

Documentation should be maintained alongside development instead of after project completion.

---

## Major Takeaways

This project taught me:

- Aircraft weight is critical.
- Material selection directly affects flight capability.
- Proper planning reduces rework.
- Testing individual systems prevents larger failures.
- Documentation is part of engineering.
- Iteration is a normal part of aircraft development.

Most importantly, I learned that a failed design is not wasted effort. Every mistake provides information that improves the next version.
