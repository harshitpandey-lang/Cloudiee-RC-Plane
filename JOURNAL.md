# Development Journal

## May 19, 2026 – Wing Construction

Today I started building my first RC plane. Since my original project was getting delayed because of components, I decided to move ahead with building an RC aircraft to learn the basics of aerospace systems and RC electronics.

Work Completed:
- Designed and built the main wing section
- Wing dimensions: approximately 80 cm × 15 cm
- Collected all required electronics and tools
- Planned the overall aircraft structure

Observations:
- This is my first experience building an RC aircraft from scratch.
- The wing structure was larger than expected and required careful alignment.

Time Tracked: 4h 51m

---

## May 20, 2026 – Fuselage and Electronics Integration

Today I completed the main body of the aircraft and started integrating the electronics.

Work Completed:
- Built the fuselage
- Mounted major electronic components
- Connected motor, ESC and receiver
- Performed initial thrust testing
- Completed aircraft assembly except final wing installation

Observations:
- I used a sunboard sheet measuring 81 cm × 60 cm for the structure.
- The material is much heavier than Depron or Styrofoam.
- Because of the increased weight, I am unsure whether the aircraft will generate enough lift to fly successfully.

Lessons Learned:
- Material selection is extremely important in aircraft design.
- Lower weight materials can significantly improve flight performance.

Time Tracked: 2h 53m

---

## May 21, 2026 – Final Assembly and System Testing

Today I completed almost all remaining work on the aircraft.

Work Completed:
- Attached the wings to the fuselage
- Created a custom XT60 and alligator clip connector for battery connection
- Tested servos, motor and wiring
- Verified control surface movement
- Started documenting the project and creating the GitHub repository

Testing:
- Motor test completed successfully
- Servo movement verified
- Electronics powered correctly
- Wiring checked and secured

Observations:
- The aircraft is now almost flight ready.
- The wing alone weighs approximately 346 grams.
- Combined with the rest of the airframe and electronics, the overall weight may be too high for successful flight.
- If this design does not fly, I will rebuild it using 5 mm Depron sheet, which is significantly lighter than sunboard.

Next Steps:
- Perform balance and CG checks
- Conduct maiden flight testing
- Record flight results
- Redesign using lighter materials if necessary

Time Tracked: 53m
