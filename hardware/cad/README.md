# CAD Files

No CAD or STEP files were created for this version of the aircraft.

The airframe was designed manually using:
- Physical measurements and sketches
- Traditional construction techniques
- Hands-on prototyping and iteration

## Design Process

1. Conceptual sketching of wing and fuselage dimensions
2. Manual measurements: 80cm × 15cm wings, 58cm fuselage length
3. Material selection: Sunboard and Depron sheets
4. Iterative prototyping with physical testing

## Future CAD Development

For next iterations, the following CAD models could be created:
- 3D airframe models in Fusion 360 or FreeCAD
- Wing profile analysis
- Component mounting brackets
- 3D printable parts (battery box, motor mount, servo horns)
- Fuselage cross-section designs
- Assembly guides and exploded views

CAD models would be particularly useful for:
- Documenting the design for reproduction
- Optimizing part placement
- Analyzing aerodynamics
- Planning for automated manufacturing
- Creating detailed assembly instructions
